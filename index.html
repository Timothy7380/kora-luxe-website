Hair Care Notes

How to wash a bone straight wig

Care3 min read

Keep the silk and the shine with a gentle routine every 10 to 14 wears.

Read More
  1. Detangle dry with a wide-tooth comb, ends first.
  2. Wash in lukewarm water with sulphate-free shampoo, stroking downward.
  3. Condition for 10 minutes, rinse cool, blot with a towel.
  4. Air dry on a stand, then flat iron on medium heat.

Keeping pixie curls defined

Curls2 min read

Curly units need water and leave-in, never a dry brush.

Read More
  1. Mist with water and leave-in conditioner before styling.
  2. Finger-detangle in sections.
  3. Scrunch in a light curl cream.
  4. Store on a stand so the curls don't flatten.

Choosing your length and lace

Guide2 min read

Bob, mid-length or 26 inches? Match the length to your routine.

Read More
  1. 10–12": easy everyday bobs, light on the head.
  2. 18–22": versatile, works up or down.
  3. 26"+: statement length, choose 250g or more.
  4. Pick a 13×6 frontal if you love side and middle parts.
